# Bitcoin New ($BTCN): 10 TikTok scripts

Each script is 20 to 35 seconds, vertical, filmed as a screen recording plus your voice
(or a text-to-speech voice). The numbers were read on BNB Chain on 10 November 2026:
re-check them on BscScan before you film, and say the number you see on screen.

**Rules for every video**

- The answer never appears before second 15.
- One fact per video. Film the proof live, never a screenshot.
- Big burned-in captions: most people watch with the sound off.
- Music that builds, then half a second of silence right before the reveal.
- No price talk, no "x100", no "you will earn". End caption always includes:
  `Meme token. Not financial advice. Crypto is risky.`
